# Turn spoken thoughts into useful text

xx Voice Input is an iPhone voice keyboard for messages, email drafts, notes, and AI prompts. Use your own API key for optional text processing.

[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/xx-voice-input/id6773677051) · [Setup guide](../help-en/) · [1.0.5 privacy](../privacy-1.0.5-en/) · [中文](../product/)

## Pick a mode for your task

- Transcribe directly.
- Polish spoken wording.
- Translate into your selected language.
- Organize a spoken idea into an AI prompt.

AI features require a configured compatible API service. Third-party usage is billed separately. Availability depends on the released version, device, and configuration.

## Your provider, with a clear data flow

Configure speech recognition and AI text processing separately. Choose which services receive your audio and text. BYOK gives you choice; it does not provide anonymity or override a provider's data policies.

## Start with one successful dictation

Set up the keyboard and permissions, try direct transcription, and then add AI processing when you need it. [Follow the guide](../help-en/).
